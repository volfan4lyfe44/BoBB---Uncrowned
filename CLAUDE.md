# Working on this novel

- The author does most of the writing or heavy outlining. Claude cleans up and polishes the author's words. Keep the author's voice, word choices, and structure; do not replace drafts with new prose unless asked.
- When polishing, change grammar, clarity, rhythm, and consistency. Flag larger suggestions (cuts, reordering, plot or character changes) as notes instead of making them.
- Check names, aliases, and facts against the Codex in `codex/` before editing a chapter.
- Manuscript files live in `manuscript/<part>/chNN-title.md`. Keep chapter numbers in file names so they sort in reading order, and update the status table in `README.md` and `planning/chapter-summaries.md` when a chapter changes.
- Novelcrafter is the writing tool. The original exports in `source/` are backups; do not edit them.
