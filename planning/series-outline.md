# Series Outline

A planned three-book series. This is a skeleton built only from decisions made so far. Gaps are marked TBD for the author to fill.

## Book 1: The Bastion of Broken Banners

**Status:** Drafting. Prologue and Part 2, Chapters 1 and 2 in progress.

- Prologue: the deaths of Hale, Shaw, Gordon, Scholl, and Bray, each entered among the Uncrowned.
- Bray wakes in Eirion and begins searching for answers.
- The Last Cohort is introduced. Toussaint is the voice of reason to Spartacus.
- Toussaint outmaneuvers Napoleon in battle.
- **Climax:** Napoleon captures Toussaint and offers him a seat in the Covenant. Toussaint refuses. In captivity he learns hints of the thing Alexander guards and fears most. Jean Lannes rescues him and reveals himself to Napoleon.
- **Ending:** Toussaint casts the deciding vote, and the Cohort and the Uncrowned band together to uncover what Alexander fears.
- Bray's arc in Book 1: TBD. Proposed in the [Book 1 plot outline](book-1-plot-outline.md#peyton-moose-bray-primary-pov): from walking away from everything to standing with others on his own terms.
- Payoff: reveal Hale's true last words, set up in Chapter 1 ("But that wasn't the last thing he said."). Placement TBD. Proposed: Chapter 31, at the sergeant's cairn (see the [scene breakdown](book-1-scene-breakdown.md#ch-31-the-cost-bray)).

A full chapter-by-chapter proposal for Book 1 is in the [Book 1 plot outline](book-1-plot-outline.md), with a [continuity and fact check](book-1-continuity-check.md) behind it.

## Book 2: Title TBD

- Napoleon deals with Lannes's betrayal.
- The alliance follows quests across Eirion to uncover its mythology and the thing Alexander fears.
- **Ending:** The Gate of Still Flame is revealed as a way to move on and accept the fates that waited on Earth. No one who crosses comes back, and the Gate turns away souls who are not ready.
- Bray's arc in Book 2: TBD.

## Book 3: Title TBD

- The Last Cohort crosses the Gate before the Uncrowned.
- The Uncrowned face Alexander. Resolution: TBD.
- **Ending:** Bray becomes the Watcher of the Gate. He offers passage to any soul who wishes to cross, and holds that the eternal ones should not interfere with the natives of Eirion if it can be helped.
