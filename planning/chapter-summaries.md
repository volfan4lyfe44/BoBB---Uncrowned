## Part 1: Prologue, The Last Breath

### Chapter 1: The First Flame

Soldiers, wearing red coats, bind the hands of a captured spy, who had been educated at Yale and trained by the Continental Army. Before his execution, the spy regrets not doing more for his country but stands firm in his conviction. As the rope tightens, a flicker interrupts the expected fall. The spy steps into a silence filled with an unspoken language and a calming voice reassures him that he was not wrong but his work was not yet done.

### Chapter 2: The Banner that Fell

Colonel leads the first all-Black regiment in the Union Army against Fort Wagner. They charge the fortified walls despite facing prejudice and hostility. He is struck and killed during the assault but his sergeant catches the regiment's flag before it falls. A presence commends his sacrifice and assures the Moral Compass that he will rise again.

### Chapter 3: The Last Sentinel

The general watches the sun set over Khartoum, knowing the city will fall to the Mahdi’s forces. He has sent everyone he could away, choosing to remain to honor his duty. The relief column is still two days away. He receives a final dispatch and writes a message to God. As the Mahdists attack, the general descends from his palace and stands facing them. He refuses to draw his revolver and is struck by a spear. As he dies, he hears a voice and sees a man in light.

### Chapter 4: A Flower in Bloom

Sophie stands trial for high treason alongside her brother and their friend. The judge sentences them to death. Sophie tells the court that somebody had to make a start before being escorted away. In her prison cell, Sophie writes a final letter. As she is led to the guillotine, she whispers "The sun still shines." A mysterious presence intervenes and tells her her courage will not be wasted.

### Chapter 5: The Unwritten Ending

On his second tour, Peyton Bray and his platoon move out to clear a suspected Viet Cong village. During the operation, Bray is shot and killed. His death is reported, but the circumstances are unclear. A presence tells him some wars continue beyond the battlefield and some truths remain hidden. Bray, alone among the five, doubts it. Bray's name is recorded among the Uncrowned, not for his death, but for his potential.

## Part 2: The Bastion of Broken Banners

### Chapter 6 (Part 2, Chapter 1): The Grey Waking

Peyton wakes up in a strange, grey landscape with no memory of how he got there. He notices the harsh air and a strange, crystalline substance covering the ground. He remembers his service in Vietnam and basic survival necessities as he tries to find direction and shelter. He thinks about past experiences like football and memories of his colleagues. He finds a shelter with an ancient inscription warning of slavery and a tyrant. Peyton builds a fire and drinks from a water source, still searching for answers in the desolate expanse. He falls asleep with dreams of violence and wakes with a fear that his survival wasn't meant to be.
