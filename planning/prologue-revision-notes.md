# Prologue Revision Notes

Decisions made while revising Part 1, The Last Breath. Use these to keep the five chapters consistent.

## Series-wide decisions

- **The presence is one entity, seen differently.** The same being meets every soul. Each one perceives it through their own life, but one constant detail repeats in every chapter so readers connect them. Signature (confirmed): leaves moving in a windless space, taken from Chapter 1.
- **Hale's true last words are a later reveal.** Chapter 1 keeps "But that wasn't the last thing he said." The payoff belongs in the Book 1 outline, ideally at a turning point.

## Chapter 1: The First Flame

- Fixed "allude" to "elude" and tightened the opening paragraph.
- Kept the captain's private kindness and added the provost marshal, William Cunningham, as the cruel counterweight. He denies Hale a chaplain and a Bible and tears up his letters. This follows the traditional account.
- MacArthur epigraph verified against the 1962 West Point speech.

## Chapter 2: The Banner that Fell

- Presence signature confirmed: leaves stirring with no wind. Added beneath the music for Shaw.
- Kept both voice lines ("Your line held..." and "more than a monument").
- History fixes: most of the 54th were free-born men, some had escaped slavery; "one of the youngest colonels"; "one of the first Black regiments raised in the North"; the men refused reduced pay instead of accepting it; Boston crowds mostly cheered, with some jeers; a sergeant takes the colors from the fallen bearer (Sgt. William Carney).
- Epigraph replaced. The Randolph quote was embellished. New epigraph is from Francis George Shaw's letter to Gen. Gillmore, 08/24/1863. Check it against a printed source before publication.
- Trimmed the training and charge paragraphs; "trench" changed to "parapet."
