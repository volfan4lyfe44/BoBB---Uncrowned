# Rethink v2: Foundation Decisions

**Status:** Decided by the author, 10/19/2026. The original plan is preserved under the git tag `v1-original-plan`. Everything in `planning/` outside this folder is source material for the rethink, not canon.

## Locked decisions

| Decision | Answer |
|---|---|
| Protagonist | Peyton "Moose" Bray. It is always Bray's vision. Other key characters orbit him. |
| World | Eirion is a different world. Earth is discussed but the story is not rooted in it. |
| Magic | None. The crossing is the one unexplained element. |
| Genre | A blend of historical fantasy, military fantasy, and literary afterlife fiction. Primary category: historical fantasy. Secondary: military fantasy. |
| Theme | Historical fiction and unfinished lives. Codex themes run underneath as undertones. |
| Publishing path | Self-published. Book 1 must satisfy on its own and hook into Book 2. |
| Tropes to avoid | "Good soldier, bad cause" and "white savior." Cut or rework characters as needed. |

## Prologue chapters become interludes

The death chapters stay, moved so each one sits just before that character's first real scene. Recommended (not yet confirmed): open the book with Bray's death as Chapter 1.

| Interlude | Placed before |
|---|---|
| Bray (Ch 5) | Recommended as Chapter 1 |
| Shaw (Ch 2) | Shaw meets Bray at the gate |
| Hale (Ch 1) | Hale's first appearance |
| Gordon (Ch 3) | Gordon's first viewpoint chapter |
| Scholl (Ch 4) | Scholl's first major scene |

## Faction stakes (open)

The author's main gap: real stakes between the Cohort, the Covenant, and the Uncrowned. Proposals, not yet chosen:

1. Arrivals are the scarce resource. In Book 1, Bray is the prize all three factions want.
2. The Uncrowned hold the Hall of Names, the only record of who crossed and who left.
3. Death in Eirion is final.
4. The natives are a fourth power with their own war aims.

Recommendation: 1, 3, and 4 for Book 1; 2 for later books.

## Codex undertones

- "They do not change unless they choose to." (Eternal Ones)
- "Belief is not required. Only obedience." (Covenant)
- "Where stories go when they demand a second ending." (Creation myths)
- "If your death was not enough to finish the story, what do you do with what remains?" (Uncrowned)
