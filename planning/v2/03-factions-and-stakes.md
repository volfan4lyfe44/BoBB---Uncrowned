# Rethink v2: Factions and Stakes

**Status:** Decided by the author, 10/19/2026, unless marked **Proposed**.

## The three factions

| Faction | What it wants | Its contradiction |
|---|---|---|
| **Covenant of the Eternal Empire** | Alexander rules the natives of Eirion as a god, forever. | Tyranny by an immortal who is not a god. |
| **The Last Cohort** | Fights beside the natives to overthrow a false god. | If it wins, immortals still rule. It could become the next empire. |
| **The Uncrowned** | End the endless cycle of death and tyranny by immortals on Eirion, their own included. | Even their allies are part of the problem. |

## The Uncrowned do not exist yet

- The Uncrowned are not an established faction when Book 1 begins. They form across Book 1.
- Bray and the squad (Hale, Shaw, Gordon, Scholl) arrived in Eirion at different times, in the order they died, and are scattered.
- **Book 1 is the journey that brings them together.** Through it they discover they share a common belief, and that belief becomes the goal.
- **The goal grows over time.** Their first, immediate goal is smaller. The full aim, ending immortal rule including their own, takes shape across the series as more like-minded souls find each other. This leads to the Gate (Books 2 and 3) and Bray's ending as Watcher.

## The Bastion of Broken Banners

- A neutral refuge where the waiting dead sit and do nothing. Not a faction.
- The Uncrowned cannot form there all at once. Part of the team may come together there, and a notable part of it issues the call to start doing something.
- The Bastion is the old way (waiting). The Uncrowned are the break from it.

## The natives

- Divided. Some believe Alexander is a god, some collaborate, some resist.
- The resisters lead their own fight. The Cohort fights under their terms, not the other way around.
- Native characters already planned: the plain-watcher (native lead), the clerk (a believer who starts to doubt), the river lord (political power).

## Other stakes carried forward

- **Death is final.** An Eternal One who is killed is gone for good. **Proposed.**
- **Arrivals are scarce.** Factions compete to reach newcomers. Bray is the arrival everyone wants in Book 1. **Proposed.**
- **The Hall of Names as the only record.** Held for Books 2 and 3. **Proposed.**

## Death and history in Eirion (decided 10/20/2026)

- **Spartacus built the Bastion**, then left it to found the first cohort. See the open note in the [Spartacus Codex entry](../../codex/characters/spartacus.md).
- **Eternal Ones die rarely, and each death is remembered.** They are few and usually command rather than fight in the ranks. Each death is a named, historic event. Each cohort Alexander destroyed cost a few famous immortals.
- **Some historical figures appear as the dead.** Not every candidate must be alive in 1970. Some can appear only as struck-out names in the Bastion's records, native legends, or remembered battles.
