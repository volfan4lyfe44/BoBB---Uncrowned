# Rethink v2: Cast Decisions

**Status:** Decided by the author in a Keep, Refine, Hold, Eliminate review, 10/19/2026. "Hold" means named or absent in Book 1, available for Books 2 and 3.

## The Uncrowned

| Character | Decision | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Peyton Bray | Locked; refine | Faces complicity. His arc makes him answer for what he took part in (the village missions, Tate, the friendly fire). Sympathetic, never let off the hook. |
| Charles Gordon | Refine | Viewpoint character. His colonial past (China, Sudan) is open conflict on the page. Flaw: obedience to rules he never judged. |
| Robert Gould Shaw | Refine | Knows his men's story was told through him. Wants the 54th remembered for themselves. |
| Nathan Hale | Refine | Left the Bastion for the Cohort; the bridge between factions. Remove the "Echoes." Left the "Keep walking" note. "Someone must go" becomes Bray's volunteer moment. |
| Sophie Scholl | Keep, refine | Bray's sharpest questioner. The only main character who did not die in uniform. Pushes back on the "good soldier" myth and insists the natives speak for themselves. |

## The Covenant

| Character | Decision | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Alexander | Locked; offstage in Book 1 | Felt through his rules, envoys, and Napoleon's fear. At most one brief appearance near the end. |
| Napoleon | Keep, refine | Book 1's on-page antagonist. Keep the Toussaint rematch. His offer must be truly tempting. |
| Erwin Rommel | Hold, rewrite | Later book only. The story dismantles the "clean general" myth he tells himself. |
| Stonewall Jackson | Hold, rewrite | Later book only. If the letters idea returns, his faith is shown in service of slavery, not admired. |
| Hannibal | Hold | Named only. Rivalry with Napoleon and resentment of Alexander suit Book 2. |
| Charles XII | Hold | Named only. Thread unclear. |
| Attila | Hold | Named only. "Scourge of the Gate" may matter in Books 2 and 3. |

## The Last Cohort

| Character | Decision | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Spartacus | Keep, refine | Cohort leader, the voice that wants to strike now. Bastion-founder backstory optional. |
| Toussaint Louverture | Viewpoint, refine | Drives the plot himself; never a passive prize. Arc: does freedom need him at the center? Plan for sensitivity reads. |
| Jean Lannes | Keep, small role | Keep the reveal to Napoleon. A few scenes, not a full arc. |
| Joan of Arc | Keep, small role | The Cohort's moral voice. "No one came for me" drives the rescue push. |
| Jock Lewes | Keep, small role | Runs the scouts who sweep the plain for arrivals. Keep the "J.L." clue in Ch 7. |

## Proposed characters (from the old planning, not on the page)

| Character | Decision | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| The plain-watcher | Keep, expand | The native lead: named, with their own goal and politics, possibly viewpoint scenes. Acts on their own; Bray does not rescue them. |
| The 54th sergeant | Keep, refine | Full life and his own goal. Do not use the old death in the rescue (a Black character dying so mostly white characters can escape). Any rescue death is chosen for the story. |
| The taken arrival | Vietnamese Eternal One | A fictional Vietnamese person who died in the same war, possibly near Bray's death. Mirrors Bray from the other side. |
| The clerk | Keep, refine | A native inside the Covenant acting with his own will. His choice has consequences he decides on. |
| The river lord | Keep, refine | The natives' political power. Uses the factions for his own goals. His condition (the Cohort rules no one in the marches) becomes a binding rule. |
| The colonel | Open | Author asked for a historical figure. Recommended: Donatien de Rochambeau (Saint-Domingue expedition; killed at Leipzig, 1813). Alternative: Charles Leclerc (Napoleon's brother-in-law; died of yellow fever, 1802). Verify details against a biography. |
