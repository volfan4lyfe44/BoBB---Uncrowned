## **Eirion**

A real world, separate from Earth: a different planet or dimension with its own history and its own human people.

- **Natives:** The human population born on Eirion. They have their own kingdoms, history, and story apart from the [Eternal Ones](../lore/the-eternal-ones.md).
- **Technology:** Medieval. Bows, swords, shields, horses, and castles.
- **No magic.** The crossing from Earth is the only inexplicable, supernatural element, and it is not fantasy magic.
- **No gunpowder.** Alexander bans it. See the [Covenant entry](../lore/covenant-of-the-eternal-empire.md), section VI.
- **Arrival:** Eternal Ones appear on a grey, crystalline plain. The [Bastion of Broken Banners](bastion-of-broken-banners.md) stands on a high plain there.

See the [Eirion world guide](../../planning/worldbuilding/eirion-world-guide.md) for proposals still under discussion.
