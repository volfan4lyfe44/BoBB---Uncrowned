## **The Bastion of Broken Banners**

*Where names are carved and erased. Where choices wait in silence.*

---

### 📍 **Location in Eirion**

The Bastion stands on a high plain of slate-colored earth, where the wind whispers but never howls. There are no roads to it. No flags above it. Only a flickering haze on the horizon marks its presence—like **a memory struggling to remain vivid**.

It is the **first place souls arrive after the crossing**. And for many, it is the last place they ever leave.

---

### 🛡️ **Exterior Design and Symbolism**

The Bastion is not beautiful. It is not a monument. It is a **scarred fortress**, **patched together from the ruins of fallen causes**:
- **Walls built from Roman stone, Napoleonic brick, Carthaginian mortar**
- **Turrets shattered and rebuilt**, as if a new philosophy tried to replace the old and failed
- **Entrances once defended now left open**, as if daring fate to try again

Tattered flags hang from rusted ironwork:
- Some recognizable—Union blue, revolutionary tricolor, an eagle standard.
- Others long forgotten—handmade, burned, torn in half.

They do not wave. They hang like unfinished thoughts.

> *“Every banner here was raised for something someone died believing in. But none of them were enough.”*

---

### 🕯️ **Interior Spaces**

### 1. **The Hall of Names**

- Long, arched, echoing.
- Walls covered in carved names, **many scratched out**, others freshly etched.
- Some names are written over themselves—**identities rewritten, doubted, denied.**
- Lit not by torches, but by a soft glow that seems to come from the stone itself—**memory-light**.

### 2. **The Quiet Courtyard**

- At the heart of the Bastion, an open-air space filled with:
  - Worn statues with missing faces
  - Stone benches carved with half-legible epitaphs
  - A well of still water at the center—black and mirror-like

It’s a place for reflection. For voices not yet spoken aloud.

### 3. **The Mirror Hall**

- Deep within the Bastion, behind vault-like doors of polished steel
- A chamber lined with shifting glass surfaces—not static reflections, but images of **selves that could have been**
- Each soul must enter it alone. No one knows what is seen—only who returns, and who does not.

> *“It does not show your past. It shows your possibility.”*

---

### 🧠 **Philosophical Function**

The Bastion is not a temple. It is not a purgatory. It is a **threshold**.
- A place to **contemplate legacy**, not be rewarded for it.
- A space where **beliefs fracture and reform**, or are abandoned.
- The only place in Eirion where **one is not pursued**—by Covenant enforcers, by fate, or by guilt.

For some, it is a **rest stop before action**. For others, it becomes a **final resting place of doubt**.
