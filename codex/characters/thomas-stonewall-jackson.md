*The Burning Standard.*

**Age at Death**: 39 **Origin**: American Civil War, 1863 – Confederate General, killed by friendly fire **Role**: Commander within the Covenant of the Eternal Empire (Antagonist) **Primary Themes**: Certainty vs doubt, moral rigidity, faith as a weapon, refusal of transformation

---

## 🧬 **Character Overview**

Stonewall Jackson walks the world of Eirion **unchanged**. Not untouched—but *unwilling to be touched* by the questions the realm forces others to face.

He is a man of unshakable belief, built from **rigid faith**, **discipline**, and a conviction that divine will is as precise and unquestionable as military order. In Eirion, where ambiguity reigns and truth is earned, Jackson does not bend—**he hardens**.

Denied passage through the **Gate of Still Flame**, Jackson now serves as a **zealous commander** in Alexander’s Covenant of the Eternal Empire. He did not fall from grace—he rejected grace’s conditions. He refused to be unmade, and instead chose to *become the sword he always believed God intended him to be.*

To Jackson, Alexander is not a false god—he is **the manifestation of ordained order**, unclouded by weakness or remorse.

---

## 💭 **Personality and Philosophy**

- Speaks **softly, but with thunderous certainty**.
- Holds an almost medieval sense of justice: *If it is written, it is right.*
- Views compromise as cowardice, doubt as sin, and self-reflection as a form of spiritual decay.
- Capable of immense compassion for those he leads—*but only if they follow*.
- Has no tolerance for introspection in others. Believes souls must choose: obedience or oblivion.

> *“The Gate asked me to surrender. But I do not offer my soul to silence.”*

---

## 🔥 **Narrative and Symbolic Role**

- A **foil to Gordon**: both devout, but where Gordon’s faith seeks understanding, Jackson’s demands obedience.
- Serves as one of Alexander’s most fearsome field commanders—not out of lust for power, but out of a **righteous sense of duty**.
- His tragedy lies not in villainy, but in **moral inflexibility**: a man who could have ascended, but refused to admit his story wasn’t finished on his terms.

He believes he already knows the ending. Eirion exists to prove he does not.

---

## ⚔️ **Presence in Eirion**

- Marches at the head of disciplined, relentless formations—never chaotic, never questioning.
- His banner burns—a flag of fire, scripture-etched, impossible to extinguish.
- Wherever he stands, order follows. But so does fear.

He brings the **certainty of death**, and the **comfort of no more choices**.

---

## 🧠 Summary in a Sentence

> **Stonewall Jackson is a man who turned away from transformation, choosing instead to become a weapon for a god he could recognize—one who never asked him to change.**
