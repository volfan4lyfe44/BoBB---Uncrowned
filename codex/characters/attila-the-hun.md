*The Scourge of the Gate.*

**Age at Death**: Approx. 47 **Origin**: Eurasian Steppe, 453 CE – Ruler of the Huns **Role**: Enforcer within the Covenant of the Eternal Empire **Primary Themes**: Annihilation as legacy, conquest without introspection, the triumph of fear over faith

---

## 🧬 **Character Overview**

Attila serves not because he worships Alexander, but because Alexander **unleashes him**.

He believes in nothing—not gods, not glory, not permanence. He is what happens when a soul becomes **so fixed in motion** that it forgets how to be still. In life, Attila was a harbinger. In Eirion, he remains one.

He is sent to the places where ascension flickers, where the **Gate of Still Flame** might draw a soul too close. His task is simple: **crush hope** before it ignites.

Attila is not a general. He is **a ritual of devastation**.

> *“He does not speak in prophecy. He speaks in outcomes.”*

---

## 💥 Role in the Covenant

- Known as **The Scourge of the Gate**—his primary assignment is the **suppression of spiritual or philosophical unrest** among Alexander’s territories.
- Feared even among Covenant ranks; his campaigns leave **no structure, no narrative—only silence.**
- Believes that death is a mercy, and that unmaking is better than doubt.

He is **the purge**, the **reset**, the **storm sent when Alexander’s rule is questioned**.
