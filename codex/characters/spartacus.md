## **Spartacus**

*The First Flame.*

**Age at Death**: Approx. mid-30s **Origin**: Thrace, Roman Republic – 71 BCE **Role**: Founding figure and battlefield leader of *The Last Cohort* **Primary Themes**: Resistance without compromise, freedom as identity, unfinished defiance

---

## 🧬 **Character Overview**

Spartacus enters Eirion not as a myth, but as a man still bleeding from unfinished war. He is not concerned with philosophy, empire, or even legacy. He fights because he was **born in chains and died refusing them**—and in this world, *no empire is different until it proves otherwise*.

In life, he led a slave revolt that shook Rome’s foundations. In death, he has no illusions of victory. But he has one unshakable truth:

> **No one owns another’s story. Not gods. Not emperors. Not fate.**

He is not charismatic in the traditional sense. He is not eloquent. He is a presence—like fire under steel, quiet until struck.

---

## 💭 **Personality and Philosophy**

- **Grim. Direct. Deeply principled.**
- Sees institutions, flags, and crowns as **tools of ownership**, regardless of who wields them.
- **Respects fighters, not banners.**
- Accepts difference but has no tolerance for those who enforce dominion—whether Covenant, king, or priest.
- Hates slavery in all forms: of body, will, or memory.

> *“The chains are different. The jailor wears laurel now. But the cage is the same.”*

---

## ⚔️ **Narrative Role**

- De facto **battlefront commander** of the Last Cohort, revered as *The First Flame*—the first to light the fires of open resistance against Alexander’s Covenant.
- **Foil to Alexander**: Whereas Alexander demands order, Spartacus demands liberation.
- **Bridge to the Uncrowned**: Despite mistrust, he respects those like Shaw and Bray—if they fight their own battles honestly.

He does not seek to lead. He leads because others **believe in the way he breaks things that were never just**.

---

## 🛡️ **Presence in Eirion**

- Wears no armor but a battered leather harness; wields a **gladius chipped by time but never dulled.**
- Rallies **freemen, fugitives, and the broken**, many of whom bear no name beyond the one they gave themselves.
- His warband is chaotic, diverse, and fiercely loyal—**not to his name, but to his refusal to kneel.**

He does not march for glory. He **moves to crack the walls** others called destiny.

---

## 🔄 Relationship with Others

- **Joan of Arc**: Mutual respect, but diverging methods—she seeks moral clarity, he values *freedom even in confusion*.
- **Toussaint Louverture**: Strategic ally. Where Spartacus breaks, Toussaint builds. Their synergy shapes the heart of the Cohort.
- **Jock Lewes**: Quiet understanding—both men know what war costs, but accept it differently.
- **Shaw & Bray**: Initially skeptical of their introspection, but grows to respect their personal resolve if they act.

---

## 🧠 Summary in a Sentence

> **Spartacus is not a leader of nations, but a breaker of cages—a man who burned once in life, and now carries the fire into the world that tried to bury it.**
