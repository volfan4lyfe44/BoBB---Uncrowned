*Alexander’s Doctrine of Dominion in Eirion*

---

### I. CORE DOGMA

> “You were denied completion. I will give you eternity.” – Alexander, upon founding the Covenant

The Covenant promises **identity without uncertainty** and **power without moral burden**. It tells the dead:
- *As members of the Covenant, you will matter forever.*
- *You do not need to change to be worthy.*
- *You were gods among men in life—let death prove it.*

**Belief is not required. Only obedience.**

---

### II. WHY THE POWERFUL FOLLOW HIM

### ⚔️ Stonewall Jackson

- In life: Devout, uncompromising, and unshakably righteous.
- In death: *His righteousness has become inflexible*, unable to reconcile contradictions.
- Alexander tells him: *“You were a holy weapon. Here, you can be that again—without guilt, without compromise.”*
- Jackson **rejects the introspection required** to reject Alexander. Instead, he seeks clarity through discipline—**becoming Alexander’s sword**.

### ⚔️ Erwin Rommel

- In life: Tactician, professional, morally ambiguous figure in history.
- In death: Offered moral ambiguity or mythic greatness—Alexander offers him **legacy without moral cost**.
- Rommel becomes one of the **Strategoi of the Covenant**—his battlefield genius repurposed for a war of meaning, not politics.

### IV. THE POWER OF THE COVENANT

Alexander's strength lies in:
- **Total symbolic domination**: His followers don't evolve—they amplify their *strongest, simplest form*.
- **Unchanging command**: His empire is efficient because it's unyielding.

> “Where the Uncrowned must earn every follower, Alexander only needs you to surrender the parts you no longer understand.”

---

### V. THE UNTOUCHED FLAME

Why don’t his most powerful commanders pass through the Gate of Still Flame?

Because the Gate demands:
- *Doubt before conviction.*
- *Vulnerability before greatness.*
- *Truth before legacy.*

Men like Jackson, Rommel, and others under the Covenant:
- **Cling to their role** as savior, warrior, or genius.
- **Fear being redefined** in the Gate’s truth.
- **Choose power over peace.**

> They could ascend. But they refuse. And that refusal binds them to Alexander eternally.

---

### VI. THE POWDER BAN

Alexander forbids gunpowder anywhere in his reach, and hunts down anyone who tries to make it.

- A native with a musket can kill an Eternal One. Firearms would break the order his godhood depends on: Eternal Ones above, natives below.
- Several of his own commanders, Napoleon first among them, know how powder is made. The ban is aimed at them as much as at anyone.
- Napoleon quietly experimenting with powder is a threat inside the Covenant.
