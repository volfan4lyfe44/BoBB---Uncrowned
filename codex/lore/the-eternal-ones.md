## **The Eternal Ones**

The souls cast from Earth to Eirion.

- They do not age once they arrive from Earth.
- They are eternal on Eirion unless killed, or unless they leave by another way the story allows, such as passing through the [Gate of Still Flame](the-gate-of-still-flame.md).
- They are distinct from the natives of Eirion, the world's own human population.

All three factions are made up of Eternal Ones: [the Uncrowned](the-uncrowned.md), [the Last Cohort](the-last-cohort.md), and [the Covenant of the Eternal Empire](covenant-of-the-eternal-empire.md).
