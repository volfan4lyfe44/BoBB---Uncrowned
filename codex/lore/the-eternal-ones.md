## **The Eternal Ones**

The souls cast from Earth to Eirion.

- How they cross is the one inexplicable thing in the series: supernatural, but not fantasy magic. No one in Eirion, native or Eternal, understands it.

- **Who crosses:** the presence that meets each soul at death chooses who comes to Eirion. Not everyone who dies crosses.
- **When they cross:** at the moment of death. Souls arrive in historical order, and time on Eirion runs the same as on Earth. A soul who died in 323 BCE has been in Eirion about 2,300 years by the time Bray arrives in 1970.
- **The presence does not choose a soul's faction.** Each soul chooses its own road in Eirion: the Bastion, the Cohort, the Covenant, or none.
- They do not age once they arrive from Earth.
- **They do not change unless they choose to.** Nothing in Eirion forces a soul to grow. Some spend centuries exactly as they were at death.
- They are eternal on Eirion unless killed, or unless they leave by another way the story allows, such as passing through the [Gate of Still Flame](the-gate-of-still-flame.md).
- They are distinct from the natives of Eirion, the world's own human population.

All three factions are made up of Eternal Ones: [the Uncrowned](the-uncrowned.md), [the Last Cohort](the-last-cohort.md), and [the Covenant of the Eternal Empire](covenant-of-the-eternal-empire.md).
